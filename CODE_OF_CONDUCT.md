# Community conduct

LocalizationHub welcomes contributors across languages, cultures and experience levels. Be respectful, make technical criticism specific and constructive, and give others room to learn. Respect people working in Arabic and other right-to-left languages as equal participants.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sharing private project data and publishing credentials are not acceptable. Follow these standards in issues, pull requests, reviews and project-related communication.

Report conduct concerns privately to **support@localizationhub.app**. Maintainer DEDTYPE may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions or restrict participation as appropriate. Reports will be handled with care; do not post someone else's private information while reporting an incident.
